Frequently asked questions

What investors ask us most

Are the units part of what I already invested in my plants?

No. What you invested in your plants stays the same, under your contract. Each unit is a new $75,000 MXN contribution that funds turning part of your harvest into mezcal. You can subscribe from 1 unit up to as many as your whole harvest needs.

When do the shares become mine?

From the day you subscribe and pay for your units. The 5% annual preferred dividend accrues from that same day.

Can I sell my shares? Only part of them?

Yes, at any time and also part of them, at whatever price you agree with the buyer. As in any company, the board approves new shareholders, and Gavé and the other shareholders have a right of first refusal. Gavé Spirits is a SAPI, a type of Mexican company designed to make it easy for shareholders to come in and out.

Does Gavé have to buy my shares back?

No. Gavé has the right, not the obligation, to buy them back at subscription value plus accrued dividends. Subscription value is not a guaranteed sale price.

Is the dividend paid every year?

It accrues every year and is paid out of Gavé Spirits' profits. If a year doesn't have enough profit, it keeps accruing and is paid later.

What happens to my shares if I pass away?

They are part of your estate and pass to your heirs. You can name your beneficiaries in the contract.

Do the units cover future harvests?

Each unit funds one specific harvest: freight, distilling, bottling and sales of that lot. Your shares stay with you, but for a future harvest we would make you a new proposal.
